1. Sensory neurones carry impulses from receptors to the central nervous system (brain and spinal cord).

2. Dendrons carry nerve impulses towards the cell body. Axons carry nerve impulses away from the cell body.

3. Axon terminals pass nerve impulses to the next neurone.

4. The myelin sheath is the fatty layer that surrounds dendrons and axons. Function: It insulates the neurone and speeds up the transmission of nerve impulses.

5. It has a long dendron to carry impulses from receptors to the cell body and a long axon to carry impulses to the central nervous system. It is also covered by a myelin sheath, which speeds up nerve impulses.
